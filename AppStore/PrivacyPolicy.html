Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 19, 2026

1. Information We Collect

DailyIQ is designed with your privacy in mind. We collect minimal data necessary to provide our services.

1.1 User-Generated Content

  • User Data: Any content you create or input within the app is stored locally on your device.
  • Progress Data: Your progress, statistics, and achievements are stored locally.

1.2 Usage Data

  • App Usage: We track basic app usage locally to improve user experience.

1.3 Device Data

  • Crash Reports: Anonymous crash logs may be collected to help us fix bugs.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Local Storage: All user data is stored exclusively on your device.
  • No Cloud Storage: We do not store your personal data on external servers.
  • No Third-Party Sharing: We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with any third parties.

3. Data Storage and Security

  • Local-Only Storage: All sensitive user data is stored locally on your device.
  • Secure Deletion: You can delete all your data by uninstalling the app.
  • No Account Required: DailyIQ does not require you to create an account.

4. In-App Purchases

DailyIQ offers premium subscriptions through the App Store. All payments are processed securely through Apple. We never collect your payment information.

5. Children's Privacy

DailyIQ is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

6. Your Rights

  • Access: You can view all data stored locally by opening the app.
  • Deletion: Uninstalling the app will permanently delete all local data.
